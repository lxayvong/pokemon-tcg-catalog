Market prices are sourced TCGplayer sealed market snapshots where available. The date labeled "checked" is the research date, not a guaranteed real-time quote. Older reference prices are unverified. Product images and identifiers are tracked independently; legacy image matches remain unverified until audited. Retailer price comparisons show documented first-party listed prices, including out-of-stock products. Dash means no verified price found. Product matching cross-checks UPC, retailer/manufacturer SKU, name, variant and pack counts; ambiguous matches require review. Prices link to their sources; marketplace sellers are excluded. Third-party marketplace offers are excluded. Unverified images are never substituted for the actual packaging. Scores are subjective editorial ratings, not investment advice. Linked official/product pages provide visual references for verified products; generic photos are intentionally avoided.

Pricing disclaimer: TCGplayer snapshots are sourced from retrieved public product and expansion pages. The checked date reflects when the listing was researched, not proof that the displayed market value was updated that day. They are not live API quotes. Remaining older reference values from November 10, 2026 have not been independently verified. “Unverified” means no reliable exact-variant market value has been established. MSRP values are retail benchmarks, which may differ by release. Packaging artwork placeholders are intentional until verified exact-product photos are available.

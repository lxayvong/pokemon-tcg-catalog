iPricing & data notes
Market prices are sourced TCGplayer sealed market snapshots where available. The date labeled "checked" is the research date, not a guaranteed real-time quote. Older reference prices are unverified. Open TCGplayer search for the exact product before buying. Expanded catalog includes official-sourced products from 2025 and 2026. Product images and identifiers are tracked independently; legacy image matches remain unverified until audited. Retailer price comparisons show documented first-party listed prices, including out-of-stock products. Dash means no verified price found. Product matching cross-checks UPC, retailer/manufacturer SKU, name, variant and pack counts; ambiguous matches require review. Prices link to their sources; marketplace sellers are excluded. Third-party marketplace offers are excluded. Unverified images are never substituted for the actual packaging. Scores and buy/wait/skip signals are subjective heuristics, not investment advice. Market trends are not inferred without a verified price history. Linked official/product pages provide visual references for verified products; generic photos are intentionally avoided.
